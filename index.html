OVERVIEW

Academic profile

Professional information and research context.

Ricardo Nuno Carvalho Monteiro is an Associate Professor at the University School for Advanced Studies IUSS Pavia, Italy, and an affiliated researcher at EUCENTRE and CONSTRUCT, University of Porto.

His academic work, as documented in this CV, spans earthquake engineering, probabilistic seismic assessment, structural vulnerability and risk, seismic retrofitting, and related decision-support methodologies.
