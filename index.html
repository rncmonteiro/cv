01 / PROFILE

ABOUT

From structural response
to risk-informed decisions.

My research focuses on understanding how buildings and infrastructure perform under extreme actions — and on translating that knowledge into practical, measurable risk-reduction strategies.

At IUSS Pavia, I combine probabilistic assessment, structural modelling and multi-criteria decision-making to support the seismic assessment and sustainable retrofitting of existing structures. The work spans individual buildings and bridges through to regional portfolios and critical infrastructure.
