## Research collaborations

Selected institutional and international research collaborations

- **10/2021-today** — Collaboration with the research group of Prof. Rita Bento (Technical University of Lisbon, Portugal) on the integrated seismic and energy modelling and assessment of mixed masonry-reinforced concrete buildings. Prof. Bento's expertise concerns the numerical modelling of masonry buildings while Ricardo Monteiro contributes to the assessment of reinforced concrete buildings, as well as integrated methods for risk assessment. The research activities have led, to date, to the supervision of the PhD thesis [12] and [16], and to the journal articles [59], [80] and [84].
- **01/2018-today** — Collaboration with the research group of Prof. Shirley Dyke (Purdue University, USA) for the development of resilience-based methodologies to support post-seismic decision-making processes related to the prioritization of limited-information inspections. Prof. Dyke's expertise concerns the formulation of decision problems as integer optimisation problems, applied to minimise the expected seismic economic losses, whose estimation represents the main contribution of Ricardo Monteiro. The research activities have led, to date, to the journal article [37].
- **09/2017-today** — Collaboration with the research group of Prof. Humberto Varum (University of Porto, Portugal) on numerical modelling and seismic assessment of Portuguese historical adobe buildings. Prof. Varum's expertise concerns numerical modelling of adobe buildings while Ricardo Monteiro contributed to the seismic fragility assessment of the structures. The research activities have led, to date, to the supervision of the MSc thesis [20] and to the journal articles [19] and [20].
- **09/2015-today** — Collaboration with the research group of Prof. José Miguel Castro (University of Porto, Portugal) on numerical modelling and seismic assessment of mixed steel-concrete structures and on seismic risk assessment (through expected annual losses) of steel structures. Prof. Castro's expertise is precisely on steel structures while Ricardo Monteiro contributes with expertise in probabilistic and risk assessment of structures. The research activities have led, to date, to the supervision of the doctoral theses [2], [5] and [15] and to the journal articles [7], [9], [13], [28], [30], [33], [35], [40], [43] and [50].
- **09/2014-today** — Collaboration with the research group of Prof. Patrick Paultre (University of Sherbrooke, Canada) on the seismic risk assessment of reinforced concrete dams. The research activities have led, to date, to the journal articles [8] and [25].
- **09/2014-today** — Collaboration with the research group of Prof. Haluk Sucuoglu (Middle East Technical University, Turkey) on seismic risk assessment (through nonlinear static analyses and expected annual losses) of existing reinforced concrete bridges. Prof. Sucuoglu's expertise concerns simplified structural analysis of reinforced concrete structures while Ricardo Monteiro contributed with the skills related to probabilistic and risk assessment of existing bridges. The research activities led to the supervision and conclusion of the PhD thesis [4], the MSc thesis [19] and the journal articles [14] and [55].
